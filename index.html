Where to go, and why it matters

Road order is Kanyakumari, then Thiruvananthapuram, then Jatayu, then north to Coimbatore. Do not reverse TVM and Jatayu.

Vehicle, group, money

Innova Crysta with 7 people. Seven adults plus 10 days of bags fills the boot. Soft duffels only, not hard suitcases; a roof carrier helps. If you also hire a driver that is 8 bodies and the car is over capacity, so one of the seven should drive.

Driving rules for this loop

Two drivers, swapping every three hours. Maximum 8–9 hours on visit days; 14 hours only on Day 1. No night driving on ghats: Srisailam forest road, Tirumala approach, Ooty, and Kolli hairpins if you add them. Fuel up at every large town before a forest or hill stretch.

Srisailam ghat road is shut 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM. If you are late, halt at Atmakur or Dornala and climb at 6 AM.

Stay pattern

Book 3 rooms everywhere — two triples, or one triple and two doubles. Always confirm parking for a long MPV. Prefer hotels over temple cottages except at Srisailam, where Devasthanam rooms are the practical choice.

Do not stay on Tirumala hill with this car. Do not stay at Dhanushkodi. Isha cottages are optional; Coimbatore is simpler for dinner, fuel and parking.

What to drop, in order

Correct road order

Pune — Srisailam — Tirupati — Madurai — Rameswaram — Dhanushkodi — Kanyakumari — Thiruvananthapuram — Jatayu — (skip Kolli) — Adiyogi — Ooty — Mysore — Kolhapur — Pune.

If you add Kolli Hills, insert it only by adding a 13th day between Jatayu and Adiyogi, and accept 70 hairpin bends in a fully loaded Crysta.

Temple hours shift on festivals. Recheck Srisailam, TTD Tirupati, Meenakshi, Rameswaram, Padmanabhaswamy and Isha a week before you roll.
